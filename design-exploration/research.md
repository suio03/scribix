# Scribix visual direction research

Research date: 2026-09-03
Surface: public marketing homepage for a desktop-first web product

## Confirmed brief

Scribix turns long-form spoken video into AI-selected, user-refined, publish-ready 9:16 shorts. The homepage must make the transformation obvious, make upload the primary action, and feel fast, creative, intelligent, and trustworthy. The current warm parchment, terracotta, serif-led system is intentionally excluded from this exploration.

## Frozen homepage skeleton

Every direction uses the same information and order:

1. Product navigation and account actions.
2. “AI video clipper” positioning and the same product promise.
3. A direct long-video upload action.
4. A visible long-video → ranked vertical-clips transformation.
5. The same four-step workflow: Upload, AI finds moments, Refine, Export.

## Cross-reference findings

- Direct peers consistently put the input action in or immediately below the hero. Scribix should keep this convention because upload is the product’s real first step.
- Vizard’s appeal comes from more than purple: the whole frame feels in motion, the typography is direct, and the warm highlight gives the gradient a focal point.
- The strongest high-trust references reserve bright color for actions, selection, progress, and output status. Large areas of saturated color need a neutral counter-surface for long sessions.
- Product proof should show a horizontal source becoming several vertical outputs. Generic “AI sparkles” are weaker evidence than this spatial transformation.
- The marketing homepage may be expressive, but the eventual editor should inherit semantic color roles and calmer surfaces rather than a continuous hero gradient.

## Source-to-direction mapping

- **Prism Pulse:** Vizard for energy and in-hero action + Figma for controlled chromatic fields + OpusClip for visible video output.
- **Signal Cutroom:** OpusClip for dark video-first staging + Linear for precision and state hierarchy + Frame.io for creative-workspace structure.
- **Pop Assembly:** Figma for modular color logic + CapCut for familiar creator workflow + Klap for one-job simplicity.
- **Cinema Index:** Runway for media-led restraint + Descript for authored editing control + VEED for progressive workflow explanation.
