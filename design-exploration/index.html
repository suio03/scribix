01 / Selected

Prism Pulse

A luminous creator canvas that makes AI clipping feel fast, optimistic, and immediately approachable. View refinement ↗

Reference mixVizard energy + Figma color discipline + Opus output proof.
Signature moveA focused light field visually splits one horizontal source into vertical candidates.
AdvantageThe clearest expression of “AI magic” and the nearest match to your stated taste.
RiskNeeds restraint in the editor so the gradient never competes with user video.

02 / Precision

Signal Cutroom

A compact, video-first instrument that makes every AI decision feel inspectable and production-ready.

Reference mixOpusClip staging + Linear state hierarchy + Frame.io workspace structure.
Signature moveThe homepage is composed like a live clipping console with a visible system status.
AdvantageStrongest bridge from marketing promise to the eventual editor experience.
RiskMay feel technical or severe to first-time creators.

03 / Creator-first

Pop Assembly

A modular, hands-on creator kit where source, clips, and workflow feel easy to rearrange and own.

Reference mixFigma modularity + CapCut creator familiarity + Klap’s single-job simplicity.
Signature moveThe transformation is a set of physical-feeling blocks assembled into publishable output.
AdvantageMost approachable and distinctive for independent creators.
RiskCan skew youthful or toy-like for larger media teams.

04 / Premium story

Cinema Index

A cinematic publishing surface that frames every upload as valuable footage becoming a finished story.

Reference mixRunway media restraint + Descript authored control + VEED workflow clarity.
Signature moveA full-bleed source frame carries the promise while the selected clips form a precise side index.
AdvantageStrongest premium positioning for studios, podcasts, and content teams.
RiskLess immediately playful and more dependent on excellent real footage.

Choose one direction—or two finalists.

Judge the feeling before individual details. Pick the direction that best matches the Scribix you want to build, or choose two finalists if you want to combine a specific strength from each. My initial recommendation is Prism Pulse, with Signal Cutroom as the strongest alternative.
