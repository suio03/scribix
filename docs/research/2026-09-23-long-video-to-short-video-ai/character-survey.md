- Original response:

There are two routes: let an AI clipping tool do most of it, or do it yourself in an editor. Either way the steps are roughly the same.

**1. Find the moments worth clipping.** Get a transcript first. Most editors generate one automatically, or you can use Whisper or Descript. Skim it for self-contained moments of 15–60 seconds: a strong claim, a surprising stat, a story with a payoff, a quick how-to, or a funny exchange. A good clip makes sense without the rest of the video and hooks in the first 2 seconds. From an hour of footage, 5–15 usable clips is typical.

**2. Cut the clips.** Trim tightly. Start mid-sentence on the hook rather than on "So, um, what I think is…", and cut dead air and filler inside the clip too.

**3. Reframe to vertical (9:16).** TikTok, Reels and Shorts all want 1080×1920. For talking heads, crop to the speaker's face. For two people, use a split screen or cut between them. Many tools auto-track the active speaker.

**4. Add burned-in captions.** Most people watch muted, so this matters more than almost anything else. Use big, bold text in the middle third of the frame, a few words at a time.

**5. Add a hook and finishing touches.** A text overlay at the top ("The mistake that cost me $10k") gives people a reason to stay. Optional extras: zoom punch-ins on key lines, light background music, and a progress bar.

**6. Export and post.** Export at 1080×1920 and 30fps, and keep each clip under 60–90 seconds. Write a platform-specific caption for each one, and stagger posting rather than dumping them all at once.

**Tools**

- **Automatic (fastest):** Opus Clip, Vizard, Klap, and Descript's clip feature. You upload the hour-long video and they suggest clips, reframe them and caption them. Expect to review the picks, since they miss context and sometimes choose weak moments.
- **Manual (more control):** CapCut (free, good auto-captions), Descript (edit by editing the transcript), or Premiere/DaVinci Resolve with auto-reframe.
- **Hybrid (usually the best results):** use AI to shortlist moments, then pick and polish them yourself.

2. **Transcribe it automatically.** The transcript makes it much easier to find interesting moments.
