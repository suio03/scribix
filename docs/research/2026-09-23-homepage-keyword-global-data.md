The report date displayed in the UI was **Sep 23, 2026**; the underlying volume averaging period was not stated on the page.

The three volume columns use different models and periods. Compare terms within one source; do not average columns or treat Google Ads competition as SEO difficulty. The candidate phrases are English, but Google Ads was set to **All languages**, so this is not an English-only market estimate.

## Homepage interpretation

1. **Core homepage wording**: `AI video clipper`, `AI clip maker`, and `AI video clipping tool` describe the product directly. `AI clip maker` has the largest Similarweb estimate in this group (11K), while `AI video clipper` has the highest observed Similarweb difficulty (83). These are variants of one main clipping intent, so they do not by themselves justify three near-identical pages.
2. **Clear workflow language**: `long video to short video AI` (2.6K, difficulty 51) and `video to shorts converter` (5.9K, difficulty 67) express the input-to-output task. A dedicated long-to-short landing page can own this workflow when its examples, steps, and proof are substantially more specific than the homepage.
3. **Podcast cluster**: `podcast clip maker` (390, difficulty 39) and `AI podcast clip generator` (1K, difficulty 26) fit the existing `/podcast-clip-maker` page. Review that page's title, copy, and examples before making another podcast page.
4. **Check product and intent before promising YouTube workflows**: `YouTube clip maker` has 10.8K Similarweb volume and difficulty 52, but YouTube source-video URL import is recorded as not connected in [the product plan](../roadmap/video-product-plan.md). `YouTube to Shorts` and `YouTube Shorts maker` also need SERP review to distinguish creating Shorts from importing a YouTube URL.
5. **Separate or defer**: `AI caption generator` has meaningful demand but a captioning-specific task and difficulty 85. Webinar, interview, TikTok, Reels, and viral long tails have limited or unavailable measured demand here; `–` does not prove no demand.

## First keyword to work on

Start with **`long video to short video AI` on a dedicated landing page**. It directly describes Scribix's upload-to-clips workflow, has measurable demand in all three sources (GKP 1K–10K, Similarweb 2.6K, Semrush Global Volume 2.9K), and its Similarweb organic difficulty (51) is lower than `AI clip maker` (70) and `AI video clipper` (83). The search results include dedicated workflow pages from [OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/tools/long-video-to-short-video-ai), [Vizard](https://vizard.ai/tools/long-video-to-short-video-ai), and [HeyGen](https://www.heygen.com/tool/long-video-to-short-video).

The landing page should own the **conversion task**: input file, AI-selected moments, clip review, captions and 9:16 framing, and a finished MP4, with a clear upload action and verified source-to-output proof. The homepage should remain the broader **AI video clipper** entry point. Its current title and hero also mention turning long videos into shorts, so coordinate metadata, headings, and internal links when implementing the new page to avoid two near-duplicate search pages. Do not promise direct YouTube source-video URL import, which is not connected in the [product plan](../roadmap/video-product-plan.md).
