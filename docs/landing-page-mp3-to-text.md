## Best For / Not Best For

### Best for

- Podcasters and creators - turn MP3 episodes into transcripts, captions, show notes, and searchable content.
- Journalists and interviewers - pull quotes from recorded conversations without replaying the same section repeatedly.
- Students and educators - convert lecture recordings into reviewable notes that can be searched and quoted.
- Researchers and operators - turn interviews, focus groups, customer calls, and voice notes into structured text.

### Not best for

- Certified legal or court transcripts - use Scribix for a first draft, then have important passages checked by a qualified human.
- Songs or music lyrics - speech-to-text tools are built for spoken words, not sung vocals or dense music beds.
- Recordings with heavy crosstalk, missing audio, or background noise that cannot be reviewed later.
- Workflows that require anonymous upload without an account - Scribix uses Google sign-in so transcripts can be saved to your library.

## Accuracy / Limitations

## How Accurate Is MP3-to-Text Transcription?

Scribix can reach 99.9% accuracy on clear audio. Real-world results depend on microphone quality, speaker distance, background noise, accents, overlapping speech, specialized vocabulary, and the quality of the MP3 recording.

MP3 is a compressed format, but clear spoken recordings usually transcribe well. For best results, upload audio with steady volume, minimal background noise, and one speaker talking at a time. For legal, medical, academic, financial, or published work, treat the transcript as a strong first draft and review it before relying on it.

## FAQ

### How do I convert an MP3 to text with Scribix?

Sign in with Google, upload your MP3 file, and Scribix will transcribe the spoken audio into text. When the transcript is ready, review it in the dashboard and copy or export the result.

### Is the MP3-to-text tool free to try?

Yes. Scribix offers a 45-minute free trial with Google sign-in and no credit card.

### What can I export the MP3 transcript as?

Scribix can export completed transcripts as TXT, DOCX, SRT, VTT, or CSV. You can also copy the transcript text directly from the app.

### Can Scribix identify different speakers in an MP3?

Yes. Scribix requests speaker labels during transcription, so conversations can be separated by speaker when the recording quality supports it.

### How many languages does Scribix support?

Scribix supports transcription in 200+ languages with automatic language detection.

### How accurate is MP3 transcription?

Scribix can reach 99.9% accuracy on clear audio, but accuracy depends on audio quality, accents, noise, overlapping speakers, and specialized vocabulary. Review important transcripts before publishing or relying on them.

### Does MP3 compression affect transcription quality?

It can. Clear spoken MP3 files usually transcribe well, but very low-bitrate audio, loud background noise, music, or muffled voices can reduce accuracy. If you have the original recording in a higher-quality format, that may be better for difficult audio.

### Is my MP3 stored forever?

No. Scribix's privacy policy says uploaded audio and video files are deleted automatically after 7 days. Transcripts stay in your account until you delete them or close your account.

### Do I need an account to convert MP3 to text?

Yes. Scribix requires Google sign-in before upload so your transcripts can be saved to your library and managed from your dashboard.
