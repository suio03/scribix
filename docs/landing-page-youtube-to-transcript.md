## Quick Answer

Scribix is a YouTube-to-transcript tool for turning available YouTube captions into a saved transcript. It is best for lectures, interviews, tutorials, webinars, podcasts, and research videos that already have manual or auto-generated captions. Paste the YouTube URL, choose a caption track, import the transcript, then copy it or export it as TXT, DOCX, SRT, VTT, or CSV. If a video has no captions, use the regular upload flow with your own audio or video file instead.

## How It Works

## How to Convert a YouTube Video to a Transcript

1. **Paste the YouTube URL.** Copy the video link from YouTube and paste it into Scribix.
2. **Check available captions.** Scribix looks for caption tracks and shows the available languages.
3. **Choose the caption track.** Pick the manual or auto-generated caption track you want to import.
4. **Import the transcript.** Scribix saves the transcript to your account with timestamped segments.
5. **Review, export, or summarize.** Read the transcript, copy text, download TXT, DOCX, SRT, VTT, or CSV, or create an AI summary on a paid plan.

## Capabilities / Entity Coverage

Scribix focuses the YouTube workflow on caption import. It checks the YouTube URL, lists available caption tracks, labels each track as manual or auto-generated when that metadata is available, and imports the selected track into your transcript library.

After import, the transcript behaves like the rest of your Scribix library. You can review timestamped segments, copy transcript text, download TXT, DOCX, SRT, VTT, or CSV, and use paid AI summaries when you need a shorter version of a long video.

For videos without captions, Scribix shows a clear unavailable message instead of pretending it can extract speech from the YouTube stream. If you own the media file, use the standard video-to-text or audio-to-text upload path to create a new AI transcript.

## Accuracy / Limitations

## How Accurate Is a YouTube Transcript?

Accuracy depends on the caption track available for the video. Manual captions are usually cleaner than auto-generated captions, while auto-generated captions can include errors from noise, accents, music, technical terms, crosstalk, or unclear speech. Scribix preserves the available caption content and turns it into a saved transcript workflow; it does not improve or regenerate the audio transcript for YouTube URLs.

For quotes, legal, medical, academic, financial, or published work, review the imported transcript against the original video before relying on it.

## Privacy / Data Handling

## Is Your YouTube Transcript Private?

Scribix requires Google sign-in so imported transcripts can be saved to your account. For YouTube URL imports, Scribix imports available captions and does not download the YouTube video or audio. Your saved transcript stays in your account until you delete it or close your account.

Scribix never trains models on user data. For uploaded or recorded audio/video files outside the YouTube-caption workflow, Scribix deletes source audio and video automatically after 7 days; transcripts remain until you delete them.

## FAQ

### How do I turn a YouTube video into a transcript?

Paste the YouTube URL into Scribix, check the available caption tracks, choose a language, and import the transcript. Scribix saves the transcript to your account so you can review, copy, export, or summarize it on a paid plan.

### Does Scribix work with every YouTube video?

No. The YouTube URL workflow works when Scribix can access at least one caption track for the video. If the video has no captions, is private, restricted, deleted, or blocked, Scribix may not be able to import a transcript.

### Does Scribix download the YouTube video or audio?

No. Scribix imports available YouTube captions. It does not download the YouTube video or audio for this workflow.

### Can Scribix generate a transcript if a YouTube video has no captions?

Not from the YouTube URL alone. If you own the media file, use Scribix's video-to-text or audio-to-text upload flow to generate a new AI transcript from the file.

### Is YouTube to transcript free in Scribix?

Yes. Scribix includes 10 YouTube caption imports per day on the free plan with Google sign-in and no credit card.

### What can I export the YouTube transcript as?

Scribix can export completed transcripts as TXT, DOCX, SRT, VTT, or CSV. You can also copy transcript text directly from the app.

### Can I choose the transcript language?

Yes, when the YouTube video has multiple caption tracks. Scribix shows the available caption languages and lets you choose which one to import.

### Can I summarize a YouTube transcript?

Yes. Paid users can use AI summaries after importing the YouTube transcript into Scribix.

### Are imported YouTube transcripts accurate?

They are as accurate as the selected YouTube caption track. Manual captions are usually stronger than auto-generated captions, but every transcript should be reviewed before quoting, publishing, or using it for high-stakes decisions.
