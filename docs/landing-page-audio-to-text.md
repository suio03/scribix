## Accuracy / Limitations

## How Accurate Is Audio-to-Text Transcription?

Scribix can reach 99.9% accuracy on clear audio, based on the underlying transcription provider's capabilities and supported languages. Real-world accuracy depends on recording quality, microphone distance, background noise, accents, specialized vocabulary, and overlapping speakers.

For best results, upload clear audio with one speaker at a time, minimal room noise, and stable volume. For legal, medical, academic, financial, or published work, treat the transcript as a strong first draft and review it before sharing.

## Privacy / Data Handling

## Is Your Audio Private?

Scribix uses your uploaded audio only to generate and deliver transcripts for your account. The privacy policy says audio and video files are deleted automatically after 7 days, while transcripts stay in your account until you delete them or close your account.

You can delete individual transcripts from the dashboard, and you can delete your whole account from the account page. Scribix does not sell user data and does not run advertising trackers.

## FAQ

### How do I convert audio to text with Scribix?

Sign in with Google, upload an audio file, and Scribix will transcribe it into text. When the transcript is ready, review it in the dashboard and copy or export the result.

### Is the audio-to-text tool free to try?

Yes. Scribix offers a 45-minute free trial with Google sign-in and no credit card.

### What audio formats does Scribix support?

The uploader accepts browser-supported audio files, including common formats such as MP3, WAV, and M4A. If a file is rejected by the browser or exceeds your plan limit, the app will show an upload error.

### Can Scribix identify different speakers?

Yes. Scribix requests speaker labels during transcription, so conversations can be separated by speaker when the audio quality supports it.

### Can I export the transcript?

Yes. Scribix can export completed transcripts as TXT, DOCX, SRT, VTT, or CSV. You can also copy the transcript text from the app.

### How many languages does Scribix support?

Scribix supports transcription in 200+ languages with automatic language detection.

### How accurate is Scribix?

Scribix can reach 99.9% accuracy on clear audio, but real-world results depend on audio quality, accents, noise, speaker overlap, and specialized vocabulary. Review important transcripts before publishing or relying on them.

### Is my audio stored forever?

No. Scribix's privacy policy says uploaded audio and video files are deleted automatically after 7 days. Transcripts stay in your account until you delete them or close your account.

### Does Scribix generate summaries or translations?

Not on this page. This audio-to-text workflow focuses on transcription, timestamps, speaker labels, copying, and exports.
