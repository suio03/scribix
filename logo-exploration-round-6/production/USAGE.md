# Scribix logo — A2 Leading Rail

## Assets

- `symbol-currentcolor.svg`: implementation master for UI; inherits CSS `color`.
- `symbol-black.svg`: fixed dark mark for light backgrounds.
- `symbol-white.svg`: fixed light mark for dark backgrounds.
- `lockup-currentcolor.svg`: outlined horizontal lockup with no font dependency.
- `app-icon.svg`: fixed dark tile for favicon and app-icon exports.

## Usage

- Minimum symbol size: 24px. At 16px, use the app-icon treatment or a simplified single-rail fallback if a future platform needs it.
- Clear space: keep at least one rounded terminal diameter around the symbol.
- Use the ink-colored mark on product surfaces; violet remains reserved for actions and selection.
- Use the fixed app icon as supplied. Do not recolor it with the current page theme.
- Do not rotate, close the open terminals, equalize the two stroke weights, or add shadows or gradients to the master mark.

## Known limitation

The double-line S category is visually crowded. The unequal rail weights, shorter inner rail, and asymmetric terminals are essential to this design’s differentiation. This practical similarity screen is not trademark clearance.
