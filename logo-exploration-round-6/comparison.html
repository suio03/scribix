Logo direction comparison

Scribix: an S with two lives

Confirmed brief

What the logo must do

Must express: Scribix, video editing and content flow, clarity, and a memorable S.

Must avoid: copying OpusClip’s P silhouette, generic AI sparkles, play triangles, radial bursts, and details that fail at 16px.

Use contexts: website navigation, workspace sidebar, favicon, and app icon across light and dark themes.

Constraints: keep the name “Scribix,” retain an S-based symbol, translate the approved double-loop idea into original geometry, and leave the current violet product palette unchanged until a direction is selected.

Decision

Select one direction or at most two finalists

Choose one direction, or at most two finalists for targeted refinement. Judge the 16/24/32px tests first, then the symbol’s distinctiveness. Color has intentionally been withheld so it cannot make one idea look more finished.

My design read: A is the closest match to your reference; B carries the clearest editing story; C is the most product-specific; D has the strongest app-icon weight.
